# Surface: Gallery (/)

Mode: Operate. Members browse the shared gallery and bulk-upload from a computer (drag-and-drop, queue with per-file progress), open items in a viewer. Anti-goal: must not feel like a corporate cloud-storage tool. Login and admin inherit the world.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The gallery is an album page the group fills together, not a folder of files; refuses the white cloud-drive grid with a toolbar.
OWN-WORLD: Album cloth shell (deep bottle green) around black card pages; photos seated in four photo corners; white-ink captions with frame number and date; one gold accent reserved for pressable or in-progress things; recognizable with all content removed by the corner mounts and the committed green/black/gold.
STORY: A member understands within seconds this is the group's private album, sees what is uploading and what failed, and knows where to drop files.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim top bar (PhotoVault, Upload, Invites for admin, Sign out); beneath it a drop strip that holds the upload queue rows (name, progress, status); below, one black page of cornered thumbnails in a whole-cell grid, each with frame number and date, selected cell circled; Upload is the single gold button.
FORM: Black-Page Album, position 4 of my grounded list, assigned by the roll; seed:a50f3432382e0996. Raised by Doujin Event Catalog (frame numbers, whole-cell repack, circled selection) and Warm Consumer App (accent only on actionable). Signature move: photo corners that turn gold when a cell is selected or uploading.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
